/datum/config_entry/string/manual_wikiurlWiki the in-game guides open (wiki books, the revenant objectives link, the poll type guide). Their page names are /tg/ wiki titles, so this can point at a wiki that has them while WIKIURL (the wiki verb, the escape menu) stays the server's own wiki. Unset means WIKIURL.
/proc/wiki_page_urlLink to a page of the guides' wiki (MANUAL_WIKIURL, else WIKIURL), or null when neither is set.
